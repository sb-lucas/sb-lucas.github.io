About me

I am a Bachelor’s student in Computer Science at ICMC-USP, in the LABIC lab, advised by Prof. Dr. Diego Furtado Silva. Previously, I was a visiting researcher at the Music Technology Group (MTG) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona.

My research focuses on Music Information Retrieval (MIR), especially representation learning for musical data. Recently, I have been particularly interested in controllable and multimodal music similarity retrieval and recommendation.

I am a very curious person. My hobbies span a wide range of interests, so I am always learning about some random topic. Among them, I like cooking, fashion, gaming, martial arts, sociology, music, and whatever I’m interested in at the moment.

Music, in particular, has always been part of my life. My earliest memory is of my father listening to Charlie Brown Jr. on his computer. I have played the electric guitar since I was 11, and I have also tried a little bit of singing and music production.
